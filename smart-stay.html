How SMART STAY works

Three years of living. Then your choice.

Years 1 to 3Stay in a leased house
Year 4 onwardsOption to own your home

Once you sign the agreement, we will present multiple property options within 40 to 60 days, so you can choose the property that suits your family and lifestyle. Once you select a property, it is reserved in your name under the agreed arrangement.

At the end of the lease

You have two options.

Option 1: Own your home

  • The selected property is registered in your name as per the agreed terms.
  • Your lease amount is adjusted towards the property’s purchase price.
  • From the 4th year onwards, you start paying the applicable bank EMI.
  • You move directly from your leased house into your own house, for a smooth and convenient transition.
  • You become a homeowner without an additional upfront payment beyond the agreed structure.

Option 2: Exit with applicable returns

  • If you decide not to purchase the property after the lease period, we sell the selected property to another buyer.
  • Your lease amount is refunded along with the applicable ROI, as per the agreement.
