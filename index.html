About us

A broader view of real estate.

Real estate is more than a transaction. It is a place to live, a space to run a business, an asset to manage or an opportunity to build for the future. iGREY Holdings brings these perspectives together through services designed around different client needs.

FAQ

Helpful answers before you begin.

What does iGREY Holdings do?

iGREY covers property buying and selling, renting and leasing, property management, investment support, construction and development coordination, and asset management, depending on the service scope.

Can I enquire about rental or lease properties?

Yes. Share your location, budget, property type and required tenure. The team can confirm current availability and viewing options.

What is the lease-related insurance offering?

A property-backed insurance offering is being introduced for eligible lease customers, with an intended timeline of within 90 days from contract start, subject to the final policy terms and eligibility.

Can I list a property with iGREY?

Owners can contact the team to discuss sale, rent, lease or management requirements and the terms of the proposed service.

Can iGREY help with construction or development?

The team can discuss the project requirements and confirm the coordination or professional services available for the specific engagement.

Are property investment returns guaranteed?

No. Any projected income or return is an estimate based on stated assumptions and is not a guarantee of future performance.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Enquire through the relevant listing or contact form. The team will confirm the property’s availability and coordinate next steps.
